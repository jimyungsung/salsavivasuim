Why so short?

A dance step lives in your legs, not your notes. Ten minutes of the same basic, five days running, does more than one long class you half remember by Thursday.

Why a theme a day?

So it never feels like homework. Turns on Wednesday, shines on Friday, a whole song on Saturday with no instructions at all.

“I stopped waiting for the week I'd finally have time for classes. I just do the fourteen minutes.”Week 3 · Foundations · placeholder quote

The method

Six kinds of practice. Every routine uses some of them.

The same method behind the full SUIM programs, cut into daily portions. A part of a routine is always one of these six, so you always know what you are doing and why.

Watch

See the movement once, whole, at tempo.

Understand

Where the weight is and why the count falls there.

Train

Slow it down and build the base.

Drill

Loop it on the clock until it is automatic.

Transform

Change the shape: arms, level, direction.

Improvise

Dance it to music, your own way.

Ready for more? The full programs are one tap away, same account. Daily is where you start, and where most people stay.
